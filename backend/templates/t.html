How Deepfake Detection Works

Our advanced AI technology uses multiple detection methods to identify deepfakes with high accuracy

Advanced Neural Network Analysis

Deepfake uses a layered AI approach combining image, audio, temporal and metadata analysis to reliably detect manipulated audio/video while providing clear, explainable results for reviewers and integrators.

Our models analyze multiple aspects of media content to detect even the most sophisticated deepfakes.

Multi-Factor Detection System

We examine various indicators of manipulation including:

  • Facial motion and micro-expression inconsistencies : Checks whether predicted muscle movements match natural patterns.s
  • Blink and eye-gaze patterns : Compares blink frequency/sequence to biological norms.
  • Lighting and shadow coherence : Tests whether lighting direction and shadowing are consistent across faces and backgrounds
  • Lip-sync and audio-visual alignment : Measures the correlation between mouth movements and speech audio.
  • Compression, encoding and reconstruction artifacts : Detects re-encoding traces and neural upsampling artifacts

Continuous Learning

Our system continuously improves as it processes more data, staying ahead of evolving deepfake technologies.
